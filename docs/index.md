# Project Overview

The game is a cooperative third-person action-investigation game in which a team of SCAR operatives enters dangerous environments to investigate unexplained phenomena, identify anomalies, complete objectives, and survive increasingly chaotic encounters.

Each mission presents players with a partially unknown situation. The team must gather information, investigate the environment, determine what is happening, and respond appropriately.

Not every threat should be solved with gunfire.

Some anomalies may be hostile creatures. Others may manipulate the environment, deceive players, create supernatural events, alter reality, or generate escalating waves of enemies.

The combination of procedural missions, investigation, unpredictable anomalies, combat, SCAR-issued equipment, and multiplayer chaos should make each mission feel different.

Players do not select from a roster of predefined heroes. Instead, each player builds their own SCAR operative through appearance customization, equipment selection, and mission preparation.


## What is SCAR?

SCAR is the temporary title of the game in development.  It is derived from the primary focus of the game **The U.N.S.C.A.R. 
(United Nations Supernatural Containment And Response) Initiative**.  They are an organization formed by the United Nations
after the first world-wide encounter with supernatural forces in 2020, when an interdimensional god of chaos plunged the world
into a time of pure chaos for roughly 40 years.  Thanks to the help of an unknown group of super-powered individuals often
called "The Squatters", they defeated the threat and reversed time so that only 8 months had passed.

Following these events the United Nations met and determined that there needed to be a governing body over all responses to
supernatural forces.  They created **The U.N.S.C.A.R. Initiative**.  After its creation, the initiative decided that it
was best for the public to have no memory of the chaotic events of 2020, so they erased the memory of these events
across the world.  The world now simply remembers it as a global pandemic.
